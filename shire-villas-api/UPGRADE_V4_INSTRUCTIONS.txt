SHIRE VILLAS AI REVENUE OS V4

WHAT IS NEW
- Premium simplified Revenue Command Center.
- Landing Page Studio: edit headlines, descriptions, price CTA, brochure/video, SEO, hero and gallery images.
- Multiple campaign landing pages using ?campaign=slug.
- Image uploads stored under UPLOAD_DIR; mount a Railway persistent volume at /data.
- Flowconnect handoff layer. Shire remains acquisition/qualification/marketing OS; Flowconnect is final CRM.
- Qualified website leads, promoted organic opportunities and promoted referrals automatically queue a Flowconnect sync.
- Manual Flowconnect Sync button on every CRM lead.

FLOWCONNECT_API_KEY=<only if your Flowconnect endpoint requires bearer auth>

IMPORTANT
Flowconnect's public website confirms CRM, landing pages/forms, WhatsApp marketing and pipelines, but its exact account-specific lead-ingestion endpoint is not publicly documented. Configure FLOWCONNECT_WEBHOOK_URL using the incoming webhook/API URL available in your Flowconnect account or supplied by Flowconnect support. Do not guess an endpoint.
