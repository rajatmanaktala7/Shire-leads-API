WHAT CHANGED
- Signed Cloudinary uploads. API secret never goes to the browser.
- Drag/choose multiple campaign gallery photos.
- Replace/remove hero image.
- Heavy project video upload directly from browser to Cloudinary.
- Cloudinary Upload Widget handles chunked upload for large files.
- Embedded project film on the dynamic landing page.
- No Railway Volume and no UPLOAD_DIR are required.
- Every campaign stores only Cloudinary URLs in the database.

HOW YOUR TEAM USES IT
1. Open /app/
2. Login with TEAM_API_KEY.
3. Open Landing Studio.
4. Choose a campaign or duplicate one.
5. Click hero/gallery/video upload boxes.
6. Cloudinary opens a drag-and-drop uploader with upload progress.
7. Select/upload files from computer. Heavy videos upload directly to Cloudinary.
8. Preview on the right.
9. Click Save & Publish.
10. Public URL: /app/landing.html?campaign=<slug>

IMPORTANT
- Cloudinary account/plan upload size limits still apply.
- For large videos, use a stable internet connection.
- Do not put CLOUDINARY_API_SECRET in GitHub or browser JavaScript.
